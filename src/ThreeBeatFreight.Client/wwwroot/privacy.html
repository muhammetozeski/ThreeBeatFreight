Privacy and storage

Solo matches, custom arenas and your last 100 successful local contract records are stored in this browser under three-beat-freight-v1. Clearing browser site data removes them. Downloads contain only the game data described by their export buttons.

Private rooms

Creating a courier stores your chosen public alias, a random identifier, a hashed recovery key and a hashed session token on Cloudflare D1. A Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite cookie identifies this browser for 30 days. Do not use your legal name if you do not want to share it with room participants.

Room members see aliases, public match state, commitment status and resolved commands. Until the round resolves, each courier can read only their own plan. A recovery key allows access as that courier on another device. Keep it private; it is shown once at creation and is never added to exports.

Rooms, plans and replays remain on the service for reconnect. Automatic record expiry is not currently offered; do not submit confidential information or personal details in aliases or arena descriptions. The source repository describes storage and deployment. The service does not collect email addresses or advertise fabricated player counts.

Advertising and payment

No advertising publisher or payment checkout is currently configured. The free game requires no payment information. Advertising, if enabled later, requires an explicit choice and is not loaded on active private room boards. No sponsor inventory is sold by this product.

Infrastructure

Cloudflare processes ordinary connection information to deliver and protect the service. Rate limiting uses a SHA-256 hash of the connecting address and a short minute bucket. Application error logs record error type, never plans, session tokens, recovery keys or payment credentials.

Your controls

Use Sign out on the Friends screen to revoke this browser session. Browser site-data controls remove local game records. Keep any exported arena or replay files only where you intend to share them.
