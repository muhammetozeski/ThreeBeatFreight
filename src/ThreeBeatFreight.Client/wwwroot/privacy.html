Privacy and storage

On this device

Your last solo or bot match, custom yards and last 100 successful contract records are saved on this device when storage is available. Clearing site data removes them. Download an arena or replay to keep a separate copy.

Your courier and rooms

We store your alias, courier identifier, protected recovery key and sign-in session. A cookie keeps this device signed in for 30 days. Room participants see your alias, board, commitment status and revealed moves. They cannot see your unplayed plan.

Anyone with your recovery key can use your courier. Keep it private. The site operator can access stored room data. Rooms, moves and replays remain available for reconnecting; automatic expiry and account deletion are not offered. Avoid personal information in aliases or yard descriptions.

Saved yards and payments

Yards saved to your locker are private to your courier. Downloaded files go wherever you choose to save or share them. A subscription, when available, adds storage capacity. The checkout displays the price and terms before payment; signing out does not cancel it.

Advertising

Optional ads use your privacy choices and stay off when those choices are unavailable. Private room boards do not load ads. You can play without loading advertising.

Connection information

Our hosting provider processes connection information to deliver the service. Short-lived request limits use a protected form of your connection address. Error logs record error types rather than moves or recovery keys.

Your controls

Sign out on Friends to end this device's session. Clear site data to remove local saves. Closing a room board keeps your courier in that room; reopen it to continue.
