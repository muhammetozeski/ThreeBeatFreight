# Comparable mechanics and product boundary

The [Robo Rally publisher's rulebook](https://renegadegamestudios.com/content/File%20Storage%20for%20site/Rulebooks/Robo%20Rally/RoboRally_Rulebook_WEB.pdf) describes programmed registers, robot movement and board elements including conveyors. Its [publisher product page](https://uk.renegadegamestudios.com/robo-rally/) presents checkpoint racing and programmed movement. These are existing genre mechanics, not inventions claimed by this project.

[Frozen Synapse's publisher page](https://www.matrixgames.com/game/frozen-synapse) describes planned simultaneous turns and single-player/multiplayer modes. The [developer-authored combat explanation](https://blog.playstation.com/2014/09/11/frozen-synapse-prime-combat-basics-on-ps-vita/) explains commit, simultaneous execution and prediction of the other player's plans.

Three Beat Freight's chosen product focus is a short, non-eliminating cargo contest: three-command energy planning, carrying and intercepting actual cargo, visible resolution and a rematch loop. This is a product/genre choice, not evidence that the idea has never appeared elsewhere or will acquire users. No competitor artwork, rulebook text, maps or code is included.
